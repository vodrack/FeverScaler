FeverScaler diagnostic logs

Attach these from the game folder to bug reports, and say roughly when the problem
happened:

  feverscaler-events.log and feverscaler-events.log.previous
  feverscaler.log and feverscaler.log.previous
  feverscaler-unlock.log and feverscaler-unlock.log.previous
  scripts\feverscaler.ini

The .previous files hold the session before the last restart.

Advanced:
SlLogLevel=2 in scripts\feverscaler.ini adds Streamline informational messages.
FEVERSCALER_UNLOCK_TRACE=1 in the launch environment writes a frame timing trace
of the unlock, feverscaler-unlock-trace.csv, up to 1 MiB.
