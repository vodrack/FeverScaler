FeverScaler - DLSS for Transport Fever 3 (beta)

Requires Windows x64 and an NVIDIA RTX graphics card.

INSTALL
Close the game and extract the ZIP into the folder with TransportFever3.exe.
If winhttp.dll or winhttp.ini already exists, back it up first. To share the loader
with other ASI mods, merge their loader settings into the included winhttp.ini,
keeping DontLoadFromDllMain=0 and RTX40MFG.asi loading before scripts\feverscaler.asi.

UPDATE
Extract the new ZIP over the old files. Your settings in scripts\feverscaler.ini stay.

SETTINGS
Settings > Graphics > FeverScaler, or the FeverScaler menu (backslash key on US
keyboards, configurable).

TURN OFF OR REMOVE
Enabled=0 in scripts\feverscaler.ini turns FeverScaler off; the bundled unlock
(RTX40MFG.asi) still loads. To turn everything off, move scripts\feverscaler.asi,
RTX40MFG.asi and RTX40MFGCore.dll out of the game folder.

To remove, delete:
  scripts\feverscaler.asi
  scripts\feverscaler\
  RTX40MFG.asi
  RTX40MFGCore.dll
  FeverScaler-README.txt
  winhttp.dll and winhttp.ini (unless other mods use them; restore your backup if
    you made one)

Optional cleanup:
  scripts\feverscaler.ini
  feverscaler*.log*
  feverscaler-unlock-trace.csv
  RTX40MFG-Universal.status.json
  <user data>\feverscaler\, where <user data> is the folder with the game's
    settings.lua (Steam: <Steam>\userdata\<Steam ID>\3493540\local)

PROBLEMS
Known issues: https://github.com/vodrack/FeverScaler#known-issues
Report others at https://github.com/vodrack/FeverScaler/issues with the files listed
in scripts\feverscaler\DIAGNOSTICS.txt.
